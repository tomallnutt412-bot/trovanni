Trovanni, from the Italian trovare: to find.

Silver, gold and platinum with a past. Trovanni finds rare vintage and pre-owned pieces before they hit the scrap pile, gives them a proper once-over, and sends them back out to be worn. Better for the planet, better value than buying new, and nobody else will have the same one.

Every piece at Trovanni already exists. We rescue rare vintage and pre-owned jewellery and watches from auctions and private sellers, so they get worn again instead of melted down, and you skip overpriced new jewellery.

Our story · why vintage

The greenest jewellery is the jewellery that already exists.

Every ring, chain and watch here was made decades ago by someone who knew what they were doing. Melting it down throws that craft away, and so does chasing whatever's new this season. We'd rather keep good pieces moving: find them, check them, clean them up and pass them on to someone who'll wear them for years.
